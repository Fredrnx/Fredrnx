### 💫 About Me

After nearly 30 years working in professional kitchens, I decided at 45 to take on a new challenge: transitioning into software development.

I'm now a student at **42 Le Havre**, learning programming through the Common Core. I enjoy learning by doing, solving problems, experimenting, and building projects.

My professional experience has given me skills that I bring to this new journey: discipline, perseverance, adaptability, and teamwork.

One memorable milestone so far: my team and I won a hackathon organized by **42 Le Havre and Safran Nacelles** 🏆

**You're never truly starting from scratch: you build on everything you've learned.** 🚀
